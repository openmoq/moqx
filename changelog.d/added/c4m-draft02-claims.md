- [`auth`](/docs/config.md#authentication-and-authorization) gains ES256 public-key verification (`es256_keys`), `audiences` and `issuers` checks for the `aud` and `iss` claims, single-connection replay protection for `catreplay` 1, `moqt-reval` revalidation that ends only the affected action when its token expires (`min_revalidation_interval_seconds`, [details](/docs/config.md#revalidation-moqt-reval)), and DPoP proof validation for `cnf`-bound tokens (`dpop_token_type`; ES256, ES384 and EdDSA proofs). `moqx-issuer` gains `--auth_revalidate_seconds`, `--auth_private_key_file`, `--auth_audience`, `--auth_issuer`, `--auth_replay_protect` and `--auth_dpop_key_file`.
